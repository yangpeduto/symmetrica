    How the numbers are made

    Possession. Frame labels from the event feed (team of the last on-ball event, nobody after the ball goes out or a foul). The tracking-only model in this repo agrees on of in-play frames.

    Voronoi dominance. Every spot belongs to the team of the nearest player. Computed on every 5th frame of the match.

    Pitch control. Spearman (2018) with momentum-aware arrival times: after a 0.1 s perception delay each player accelerates at up to 7 m/s² against drag capping him at 7.8 m/s, so momentum carries him on while he brakes. Logistic arrival uncertainty (σ = 0.6 s), control rate 4.3/s, integrated until the probabilities sum to 1.

    EPV. Attacking pitch control × xT at each spot: the dangerous space the team in possession owns.

    Pass options. For every teammate: control at the receiver × (1 − chance a defender gets within 1 m of the lane before the ball). Value = completion × xT, gain relative to the ball's current xT. Validated on intercepted vs completed passes (ROC AUC ≈ 0.82–0.84).

    Off-ball runs. ≥ 1.5 s above 5 m/s while a teammate has the ball; classified as in behind, overlap / underlap, dragging a marker, or support. Space created = extra attacking control for teammates against a counterfactual where the runner (and his marker) stayed put.

    Shape and formation. Outfield convex hull, width, depth and centroid height per phase; formations by Hungarian matching of the average centroid-relative shape to twelve templates.

    Players. Distance, high-speed (> 5.5 m/s) and sprint (> 7 m/s) running on 0.4 s mean speed; top speed held for 1 s; pressures = ≥ 0.4 s within 4 m of the ball without it. Percentiles vs all outfield players with 30+ min.

    PPDA. Opponent passes divided by defensive actions (ground challenges, interceptions, tackles) in the 60 % of the pitch furthest from the pressing team's goal.

    Counter-press. Share of open-play ball losses won back within 5 s; tracking gives how many players were within 10 m of the ball in that window.

    Expected threat. Singh's value iteration on a 12 × 8 grid with kernel-smoothed counts and a geometric xG prior. Carries are inferred between consecutive touches of the same player.

    Line-breaking passes and packing. Opponent outfielders are split into lines at the largest gaps in their positions; a completed pass that starts 2 m behind a line and arrives 2 m beyond it breaks it. Packing counts opponents goal-side of the ball at the pass minus at the reception.

    Momentum. Exponentially weighted (span 5 min) difference in xT created per minute, scaled to its maximum.
