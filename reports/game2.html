    How the numbers are made

    Possession. Frame labels from the event feed (team of the last on-ball event, nobody after the ball goes out or a foul). The tracking-only model in this repo agrees on of in-play frames.

    Voronoi dominance. Every spot belongs to the team of the nearest player. Computed on every 5th frame of the match.

    Pitch control. Spearman (2018): each player's arrival time from his position and velocity (0.7 s reaction, 5 m/s), a logistic arrival uncertainty (σ = 0.45 s) and a control rate of 4.3/s, integrated until the probabilities sum to 1.

    PPDA. Opponent passes divided by defensive actions (ground challenges, interceptions, tackles) in the 60 % of the pitch furthest from the pressing team's goal.

    Counter-press. Share of open-play ball losses won back within 5 s; tracking gives how many players were within 10 m of the ball in that window.

    Expected threat. Singh's value iteration on a 12 × 8 grid with kernel-smoothed counts and a geometric xG prior. Carries are inferred between consecutive touches of the same player.

    Line-breaking passes and packing. Opponent outfielders are split into lines at the largest gaps in their positions; a completed pass that starts 2 m behind a line and arrives 2 m beyond it breaks it. Packing counts opponents goal-side of the ball at the pass minus at the reception.

    Momentum. Exponentially weighted (span 5 min) difference in xT created per minute, scaled to its maximum.
